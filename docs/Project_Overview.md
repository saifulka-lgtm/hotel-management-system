# Enterprise Hospitality Platform

## Project Vision

Enterprise Hospitality Platform is a centralized business management solution designed to integrate Hotel Operations, Restaurant Operations, Food Delivery, Inventory Management, Billing, Reporting, Notifications, and Administration into one secure and scalable platform.

---

## Project Objectives

- Improve operational efficiency
- Centralize business data
- Support Hotel, Restaurant, and Delivery businesses
- Provide real-time reporting
- Reduce manual work
- Ensure secure access with role-based permissions
